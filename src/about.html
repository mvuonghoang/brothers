The BROTHERS Story

After more than six years in the nail industry, we realized our guests wanted more than just a pretty finish — they deserved thoughtful, unhurried care and an experience that truly helps them unwind.

Known for creating the most artistic nail designs, BROTHERS was built to be more than a salon. It is a welcoming space where every guest can express their individuality, truly be themselves, and take dedicated time to care for their mental well-being.
